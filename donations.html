How Funds Are Used

Every dollar raised directly supports building a sanctuary for growth, connection, and health.

Physical Excellence

Strength & Floor

Funds will purchase professional impact-absorbing sports flooring, heavy-duty athletic racks, free weights, and modern strength machinery designed for veterans, youth, and local athletes.

Peer Support

Veteran Lounge

Creating a dedicated environment for veterans. This lounge serves as a central hub for career transition mentoring, peer support networks, health fairs, and relaxing social spaces.

Youth & Teamwork

Community Courts

Construction and equipment for multi-sport courts. This space hosts our youth basketball leagues, athletic conditioning clinics, and community recreation opportunities.

Next Level Recreation Center, LLC is a registered 501(c)(3) organization. All build-out campaign contributions are securely managed and transparently reported.
